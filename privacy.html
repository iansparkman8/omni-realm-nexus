Privacy Policy

Omni-Realm Nexus · Last updated 7 November 2026

Omni-Realm Nexus ("the game") is an independent browser game. This page explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google. Guests play without giving any personal information.
  • Game data. Your character name and appearance, position in the world, items, Flux balance, quest progress, crafted gear, explored map areas and anything you forge, list on the market or place in the world.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure page. We receive an order ID, the pack you bought, its price and whether it was paid. We never see or store your card number or wallet details.
  • Cash-out requests. If you request a cash-out, we store the PayPal email you give us, the amount and the request's status.
  • Direct connections (optional). If you turn on "Connect directly to nearby players", your device exchanges movement data directly with nearby players who have also turned it on. This lets their devices see your IP address. It is off by default. Google's public STUN servers help the two devices find each other.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and request times) for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To run the game and keep your progress, items and Flux safe across devices.
  • To process purchases and cash-outs and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To show other players your character name, appearance, position, market listings and placed creations, which are part of the shared world.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in and servers.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • PayPal, which processes cash-out payouts, if and when cash-outs are open.
  • Google, if you choose Google sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the game's website.
  • Google STUN servers, only if you turn on direct connections.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Data is stored with Supabase. Access to your account data is restricted to your own signed-in session, and money and items can only be changed through checked server functions. Some progress is also saved in your browser's local storage on your device.

Your choices

  • You can play as a guest without an account.
  • You can ask us to export or delete your account and game data by emailing the address below. Deleting your account removes your characters, items, Flux and progress.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Cash-outs and purchases require a parent or guardian where the law requires it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
