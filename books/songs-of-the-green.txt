SONGS OF THE GREEN

By Wanderer Elian

The plains stretch out like an endless ocean of emerald and gold, bowing only to the wind. To walk the grassland is to understand freedom. There are no towering cliffs to hem you in, no dense canopies to blot out the sun. Here, the sky is a vast, uninterrupted dome of blue, and the horizon is a promise, not a boundary.

We left the Haven at first light. The air was crisp, carrying the sweet scent of blooming wildflowers and the distant, earthy tang of rain. The worn path beneath our boots soon gave way to a soft carpet of grass. It is a forgiving landscape, one that invites you to wander, to lose yourself in the gentle roll of the hills.

I have traveled many roads, but the open plains always call me back. There is a profound silence here, broken only by the rustle of wind through the tall stalks and the occasional call of a meadowlark. It is a silence that speaks of peace, of a world at rest.

We camped near a lone field oak, its branches spreading wide like a welcoming embrace. As the sun began to dip below the horizon, painting the sky in strokes of fiery orange and soft purple, we lit a small fire. The crackle of the flames was a comforting companion in the vast emptiness. We shared stories of past journeys, of faces remembered and forgotten, our voices carrying into the encroaching night.

In the morning, the dew sparkled like scattered diamonds across the grass. The world felt newly born, washed clean by the night. We packed our gear and set off again, our spirits high. There is a sense of endless possibility here, a feeling that whatever lies over the next rise is something wonderful.

The grassland does not challenge you with treacherous paths or hidden dangers. It offers a gentle journey, a chance to breathe deep and let go of the burdens you carry. It is a place of healing, of finding oneself in the vastness of nature.

As I write this, sitting on a sun-warmed field boulder, I watch the wind trace patterns in the grass. It is a dance as old as time, a reminder that we are but small pieces of a grand, beautiful puzzle. The open road stretches before me, a ribbon of gold winding through the green. I do not know where it leads, but for the first time in a long time, I am not afraid. I am ready to see what awaits.

The journey is the destination, and here, in the heart of the grassland, every step is a blessing. Let the wind be your guide, and the open sky your roof. There is no greater freedom.
