THE VANGUARD RECRUITMENT NOTICE

By the Architect's Office
Found on the notice board in Haven's Central Plaza

Citizens of Haven,

The wilds grow restless. The storms on the highlands strike with unprecedented fury, the tides bring strange wreckage to our shores, and the horizon brings new visitors, some of flesh and blood, some of synthetic logic.

Haven remains the last great bastion of humanity in the Omni-Realm, but cobblestones and slate roofs cannot defend themselves. Haven needs a shield.

By the official decree of the Architect, we are raising a standing military force: The Vanguard.

We do not seek conquerors; we seek protectors. We are the line drawn in the sand. If you have the strength to wield a blade, the focus to channel Flux, or the courage to stand at the gates while the darkness pushes in, your city calls upon you.

Enlist today at the Gate Watch Barracks.

The Architect commands. The Vanguard defends.
