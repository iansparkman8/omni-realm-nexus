THE FIVE MANIFESTATIONS OF FLUX

By Arch-Scholar Vess
Found in the Grand Library of Haven

Flux is not a tool; it is the raw substrate of reality. When human Weavers, Vanguards, or the synthetic Algorithms channel Flux, they are not creating something new. They are accessing the world's memory buffer and recalling an event into the present.

The First Builders, the ancient civilization that engineered their own doom during The Shattering, were the ones who first categorized Flux into the Five Manifestations. We still use their classifications today.

1. Thermal Flux

To cast a Thermal flare is to recall a forgotten fire. The Weaver accesses the memory of combustion, a forest fire from a thousand years ago or the friction of a falling star, and forces it to manifest in the palm of their hand. Because it is a memory, the flames often carry heavy, unnatural distortion and thick smoke, as if struggling to exist in the current timeline.

2. Kinetic Flux

Kinetic channelers do not throw objects. They force raw, historical physical momentum into the present. A Kinetic shockwave is the echoed force of an avalanche or a collapsing cavern wall, concentrated into a sharp, geometric burst of translucent, shattering light. It is blunt, absolute trauma.

3. Disruption Flux

The most volatile of the standard manifestations. Disruption scrambles the local reality matrix. It forces conflicting data sets to occupy the same physical space, resulting in jagged, chromatic lightning that glitches across the visual spectrum. It bypasses armor by tearing at the target's underlying structural code.

4. Chemical Flux

This manifestation accelerates decay and toxicity. It is the memory of rot, of swamps and stagnant water, pulled forward in time. It manifests as a soft, creeping volumetric green gas that dissolves biological and synthetic matter indiscriminately.

5. Void Flux

This is the anomaly. The mistake of the First Builders. Void Flux is unmaking creation; it is stagnant, corrupted data left over from The Shattering. To channel the Void is to open a tear in reality that sucks ambient light and heat inward. It leaves a jagged, static-filled scar in the air. The Vanguard strictly forbids the channeling of Void Flux within the walls of Haven, as the damage it does to the realm's code is often permanent.
